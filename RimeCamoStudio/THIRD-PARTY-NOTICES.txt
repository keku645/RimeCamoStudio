Camo Studio carries the following third-party components inside its executable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rime (https://github.com/ModdersLink/Rime)
  RimeREPL and its Frostbite 2 support libraries, built from the same source
  tree as Camo Studio. They read the game's files from the user's own install.
